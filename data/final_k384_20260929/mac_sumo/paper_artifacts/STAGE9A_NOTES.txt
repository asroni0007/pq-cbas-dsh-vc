STAGE 9A — PAPER ARTIFACT NOTES
================================

Cross-device comparison:
- Apple M2 and Raspberry Pi 5 use liboqs 0.16.0.
- ESP32 uses the pinned PQClean clean implementation.
- Ratios are measured cross-device stack costs, not
  CPU-normalized speed ratios.

ESP32:
- Framework-sign is the length-equivalent signing-transcript
  microbenchmark used in the final ESP32 experiment.
- Timing outliers are retained.
- No Fiat-Shamir rejection-loop counts were instrumented
  directly; timing tails must not be assigned a causal
  rejection-count interpretation.

Execution-time budgets:
- 5 msg/s  -> 200 ms signing budget
- 10 msg/s -> 100 ms signing budget
- 20 msg/s -> 50 ms signing budget
- These are signing-budget exceedances, not network deadline
  misses and not RSU-arrival-to-verdict measurements.
