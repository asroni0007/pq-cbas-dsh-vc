======================================

Controlled rerun:

A second SUMO execution was performed to test reproducibility.
Raw FCD file SHA-256 values can differ because run/output metadata
may differ. Reproducibility is therefore evaluated from canonicalized
timestep and vehicle trajectory content rather than raw XML bytes.

Historical artifact values:
- 4943 vehicles/hour
- 3193 vehicles in RSU
- mean contact 68.4 s
- p95 contact 193 s

The original studyarea_fcd.xml supporting those historical values is
not present in the available artifact. Therefore the historical
mobility statistics are retained only as archived results, while the
controlled rerun values above are used for the revised evaluation.

Reproducibility decision
------------------------
The primary no-explicit-seed trace was reproduced in a second SUMO 1.26.0 run.
After canonicalization of timestep and vehicle trajectory contents, both runs
produced the same trajectory SHA-256, 3600 timesteps, 445778 vehicle
observations, and 4402 unique vehicles.

A separate two-run --seed 42 sanity check was also performed. It is retained
only as a reproducibility control and is not used as the final mobility trace.
